FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know regarding bespoke ordering, global express shipping, and concierge assistance.

How do I request custom architectural sizing or bespoke alterations?
Our atelier provides bespoke tailoring for all haute couture garments. Simply mention your specific body measurements or size profile in the contact message above or book a private fitting consultation. A senior master tailor will contact you within 24 hours.
What are the shipping times and duties for international orders?
All orders are dispatched via priority express courier with signature required upon delivery. Standard delivery timeline is 2-4 business days worldwide. All import duties, taxes, and luxury tariffs are fully included at checkout.
How does the complimentary 30-day doorstep return concierge work?
Every STACKLY purchase includes complimentary doorstep return pickups. You can request a pickup directly through your Client Dashboard under 'My Orders' or contact our concierge team. A courier will collect the package from your specified address free of charge.
How can I obtain an authenticity certificate for limited edition drops?
Each piece from our limited Monolith and Avant-Garde drops carries a unique digital NFC cryptographic seal and a physical signed certificate of authenticity from the Paris Atelier.
