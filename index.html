FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I choose my size?

Our garments feature accurate international sizing. Consult our detailed size guide on each product page for exact shoulder, chest, and length measurements.

What payment methods are available?

We accept all major credit cards, UPI, Apple Pay, Net Banking, and express checkout options.

How long does delivery take?

Standard express shipping takes 2-4 business days. Priority overnight concierge delivery is available at checkout.

Can I return an item?

Yes, we offer complimentary 30-day returns for unworn items with original tags intact.

How do I track my order?

Log into your Client Portal dashboard to view real-time tracking progress from packaging to final delivery.

Are products authentic?

Every STACKLY creation is guaranteed 100% authentic, accompanied by a digital serial certificate.
